# G2002 — property spelled in another dialect

The word exists — in WPF, CSS or Godot — but gogo deliberately spells the
concept differently, and refuses to guess which of several meanings you
wanted.

## Why this is an error (and not a silent alias)

Fuzzy matching can't bridge `foreground` → `font_color`, and auto-applying
the nearest guess can be *worse* than failing: `color=` applied as a
modulate tint would recolor the whole control, children included, and look
almost right. gogo routes you to the exact spelling instead.

## The signposts

| you wrote | you probably meant |
|---|---|
| `foreground=` | text: `font_color=` · fill: `background=` |
| `text_color=` | `font_color=` (pairs with `font_size=`) |
| `color=` | text: `font_color=` · fill: `background=` · whole-control fade: `opacity=` |

Only nodes that own a literal fill (`Rectangle`) have a real `color=`.

## Fix

Use the gogo spelling from the table. REFERENCE.md "Properties" lists the
whole vocabulary.
